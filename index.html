WHAT IS THRIVE

THRIVE is a goal tracker that looks and sounds like a 1980s phosphor terminal. It started with fridge magnets: a paper calendar where you move a magnet onto the day you actually kept your promise. THRIVE keeps that honesty and adds the one thing paper cannot — it shows you what a year of small daily decisions adds up to.

Check in on real things — workouts, reading, calling your parents — and watch six sides of your life gain levels. Nothing here is pretend: the stats are yours, the progress is yours, and the only opponent is your own week.

Everything lives on your device. No account, no feed, no ads, no analytics.

HOW IT WORKS

  • Check-ins & XP. Every check-in grants XP twice — to the goal's stat and to you.
  • Stat levels pay off. The higher a stat's level, the more XP each of its check-ins gives — from 5 XP at level 1 to 30 XP at level 10.
  • Weekly streak bonus. Close every scheduled day of a goal's week and Monday brings a bonus. Consecutive weeks multiply it: ×2, ×3, ×4…
  • Decay. A stat idle for 7 days starts fading. A single check-in stops it — real life, honestly simulated.
  • Shields. A shield covers a missed day so it doesn't break your streak. You start with 3 and earn more as you climb.
  • Ranks. Your XP builds your level; every 10 levels opens a new tier with a new rank insignia.

HOW IT'S DRAWN

The interface is drawn, not imported. Every stat glyph, every rank insignia, the radar chart, the scanlines and the vignette are procedural — there is not a single bitmap in the UI. Pick your phosphor (green, amber, blue, cyan or red) and your scanline pattern, and the whole screen recolours on the spot.

Overlays type themselves out with a blinking cursor. Switching tabs flickers like a tube re-syncing. Nine sound effects were recorded for it. Built and designed by one person, with no third-party code.

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: July 13, 2026

THRIVE is built to know nothing about you.

  • No data collection. THRIVE does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.
  • Local storage only. Your goals, check-ins, stats and progress live solely on your device (and in your personal device backups, if you have them enabled). We have no servers and no access to your data.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • Notifications. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to us or anyone else.
  • Data deletion. Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your device.
